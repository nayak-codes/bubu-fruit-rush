🏢 About Balanju Solutions

Crafting Fun, Smooth & Family-Friendly Mobile Games

Who We Are

Balanju Solutions is an independent game development studio dedicated to creating high-performance, vibrant, and engaging mobile arcade experiences. Our goal is to bring classic arcade joy into modern mobile devices with 60 FPS smooth physics, polished UI design, and wholesome gameplay for players of all ages.

The Story of Bubu Fruit Rush

Bubu Fruit Rush was born out of a passion for classic reflex arcade games! We wanted to create a character that players fall in love with—enter Bubu the cute Panda. Combined with 30 handcrafted campaign levels, dynamic fruit combo multipliers, power-ups like Magnet & Time Freeze, and zero intrusive pay-to-win barriers, Bubu Fruit Rush delivers endless fun right at your fingertips.
