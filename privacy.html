🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2026 • App: Bubu Fruit Rush • Developer: Balanju Solutions

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bubu Fruit Rush, developed by Balanju Solutions. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our mobile game and website. This Privacy Policy outlines how data is handled when you download, install, or play Bubu Fruit Rush.

2. Information Collection & Zero Personal Data Policy

Bubu Fruit Rush does NOT require user registration, account login, or personal identity submission.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect, store, or harvest names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, GPS locations, or financial details.
  • Local Storage & Game Progress: All game scores, 30 campaign level progress, sound preferences, and unlocked Panda skins are saved locally on your device (using SharedPreferences / LocalStorage). This data stays 100% on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Bubu Fruit Rush is a family-friendly 2D arcade game suitable for players of all ages.

  • We comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR standards.
  • We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
  • There are no public chat rooms, social media sharing feeds, or user profile publishing features within the game.

4. Third-Party Diagnostic Services & Analytics

The app may utilize standard Google Play diagnostic services (such as Google Play Games Services or Google Play Crash Reporting) to ensure app stability and smooth performance. These standard framework services collect non-identifiable system diagnostics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Developer Privacy Guidelines

5. Data Security

Because your high scores and campaign progression are stored locally on your device, your data is as secure as your personal smartphone. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data will reset your local scores.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be posted directly on this official web page with an updated effective date.
